# Block Inventory

Survey of available blocks for content authoring decisions.

## Local Blocks (Already Available)

### Standard Blocks
- **hero**: Large heading, text, and buttons at top of page
- **cards**: Grid of items with images, headings, and descriptions
- **columns**: Side-by-side content in 2-3 columns
- **accordion**: Expandable questions and answers
- **tabs**: Content organized in switchable tabs
- **carousel**: Rotating images or content panels
- **quote**: Highlighted testimonial or pullquote
- **fragment**: Reusable content section
- **table**: Tabular data display
- **video**: Embedded video player
- **embed**: Embedded third-party content
- **form**: Interactive forms
- **modal**: Popup/dialog content

### Custom Variants (From Homepage Migration)
- **hero-pharma**: Pharmaceutical hero with Relief branding, product name, and condition messaging
- **cards-condition**: Condition selector cards with severity levels and medical notation
- **columns-product**: Product images displayed side-by-side (RINVOQ bottle and oral solution)

### Infrastructure Blocks
- **header**: Site navigation (auto-populated)
- **footer**: Site footer (auto-populated)
- **search**: Search functionality
